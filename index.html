Oahu, Hawai'i

The Gathering Place

Oahu is the main third island in the island chain that forms the state of Hawai'i.

Oahu is about 3.8 million years old. It formed from under sea volcanos over a span of approxiatly 2 million years

Facts about Oahu

  • Oahu is the Third Largest of the Hawaiian Islands
  • Roughly 75% of Hawai'i's population live on Oahu
  • Some of the famous landmarks include Pearl Harbor, Iolani Palace, and Kualoa Ranch
